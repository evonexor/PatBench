# Known limitations and open items

The limitations are ordered by whether they can affect conclusions drawn from the data. The numbers describe the reference release r6 (frozen 2026-11-01) unless a statement is labelled "(verified on r4; not repeated on r6)". The first two items are work for people, not something code can supply.

## Manual verification that has not been done

1. **The target mappings of the test groups have not been reviewed by a person.** In r6 the time-split test sets of `rank_ic50` (172 groups) and `rank_ki_kd` (33 groups) form 205 distinct groups, listed in `target_review_test.tsv`. Another 39 test groups of `rank_highcontrast`, `rank_complex_mutant` and `rank_ec50` are listed in `eval_support/target_review_test_other_tasks.tsv`. Together these are 244 distinct test groups, and none has been checked group by group.
   - The automated checks classify 113 of the 205 groups as a strict subset (high confidence, the printed label names the target, and the public-database cross-reference does not disagree). The other automated counts for the 205 groups were computed on 2026-11-02 from `target_review_test.tsv` and `eval_support/test_group_profile.parquet` of the frozen r6 directory: 139 groups have high and 66 medium confidence; the printed label does not name the target in 46 groups; the public-database cross-reference agrees in 142 groups, disagrees in 6 and is absent in 57; the strict subset has 113 groups, 4 of them in the `novel` tier. (For comparison, on r4 with 199 groups: 136 high, 63 medium, 46 label not naming the target, 6 disagreements, strict subset 110 groups with 5 in the `novel` tier.)
   - The review page can be generated with `pipeline/11_support/review_page/` (r6 page: `target_review_r6.html`). Until the review is done, the accuracy of the mapping is known only from the validation sample (target: 85.8%, see [VALIDATION](VALIDATION.md)).
   - A wrong mapping affects only models that use protein information; the within-group ranking labels do not depend on it.
2. **The 61 annotation disagreements have not been adjudicated.** In the validation sample, 61 (patent, target) pairs were judged differently by the two annotators. Their rows (14,386) are held out as `pair_under_adjudication`, and `settled_by_adjudication` in `release_manifest.json` is 0. In addition, 85 items that both annotators judged wrong (80 pairs, 12,223 rows, `pair_validated_error`) and 129 patents held out as a whole (54,292 rows, `patent_has_validation_issue`) are outside the task sets. Both annotators were automated (LLM-based) sessions; no domain expert took part.

## Data

3. **Most test rows are already in the public databases, and the `novel` tier is small.** In r6, 84.3% of the test rows of `rank_ic50`, 82.1% of `rank_ki_kd`, 83.2% of `rank_highcontrast` and 83.5% of `rank_ec50` are in BindingDB or ChEMBL (65.3% for `rank_complex_mutant`). In r5 the figure was 67-70%; after the structure repair, repaired structures can be matched to public molecules, and the `novel` tier became smaller as a result. The `novel` tier (no row of the group in the public data) has `rank_ic50` 26 groups from 24 patents, `rank_ki_kd` 6 groups from 2 patents, `rank_highcontrast` 30 groups from 24 patents, `rank_complex_mutant` 4 groups from 4 patents and `rank_ec50` 1 group from 1 patent. Among all `split_time == test` groups of `sar_all.parquet`, the number of `novel` groups fell from 474 (r5) to 396. A model pretrained on these databases has to be reported by `test_tier`; results in the `reference` tier may reflect memorisation. The `novel` tiers of `rank_ki_kd` and `rank_ec50` are suitable for exploration only. `eval_all/` provides larger zero-shot sets (189 `novel` groups for `rank_ic50`, 56 for `rank_ki_kd`, 245 deduplicated for both).
4. **The test sets are small and their chemistry is dated.** Test groups / patents in r6: `rank_ic50` 172 / 136, `rank_ki_kd` 33 / 21, `rank_highcontrast` 182 / 137, `rank_complex_mutant` 29 / 15, `rank_ec50` 9 / 3. In r6, the five largest patents of `rank_complex_mutant` account for 19 of its 29 test groups (65.5%); over all 72 groups of the task the share is 27.8% (computed on 2026-11-02 from `tasks/rank_complex_mutant.parquet`).
   - The test chemistry was mainly filed in 2016-2018 (priority year) and granted in 2020-2025. Grants of 2025 cover only part of that year (r4 and r5 documentation). Of the 199 `rank_ic50` and `rank_ki_kd` test groups of r4, 2 had a priority year of 2022 and none a later one (verified on r4; not repeated on r6). Extending the test set requires adding published patent applications; this has not been done.
   - Statistical resolution (patent resampling, 80% power, from the r6 freeze note): the whole `rank_ic50` test set (172 groups) resolves a difference in the median within-group Spearman of about 0.05 (when the differences are consistent across groups) to 0.1 (for realistic model pairs); its `novel` tier (26 groups) needs 0.15 or more; `rank_ki_kd` (33 groups) resolves about 0.1-0.19. The `novel` tier of `rank_ki_kd` (6 groups, 2 patents) and `rank_ec50` (3 patents) cannot support conclusions on their own.
5. **Groups of the same patent are not independent.** Kinase selectivity panels and similar tables produce several groups with shared molecules and strongly correlated rankings. Confidence intervals should resample patents. The build-time `evaluate.py` resamples groups; patent resampling widens the interval (measured with r4 baselines: heavy-atom baseline on `rank_ic50`, width 0.147 to 0.163; logP baseline on `rank_ki_kd`, 0.135 to 0.182; medians unchanged; verified on r4; not repeated on r6).
6. **The structure repair of r6 is incomplete.** 1,953 MOL-file records could not be expanded (peptides, polymers, antibodies, placeholders, rare abbreviations, exporter-damaged superatoms). They correspond to 1,603 patent-molecule pairs and 3,678 rows that keep the flag `structure_alias_suspect` (r5: 15,736 rows) and stay outside the task sets. By label text, 1,015 files (2,255 rows) are peptides, polymers or antibodies, 721 files (1,150 rows) have labels that look like small-molecule groups, and 217 files (273 rows) are exporter defects. Three further records (nitrile conflicts between drawing and text) are quarantined; they are in no task, and the derived table `structure_repair/nitrile_audit/sar_all_reviewed.parquet` flags 3,681 rows.
   - A trial with additional rules (TBS, Trt, Ts, Me3Si, Bu3Sn, NHMe, NHBoc and others) would resolve only 146 of the 1,953 files (61 patents, 277 `sar_all` rows) and would change rows in 6 existing task groups (2 each in `rank_ic50`, `rank_highcontrast` and `rank_complex_mutant`; 2 of them test groups). Only 42 of these files can be checked against public databases: 52.4% match, against 66.0% for a control of molecules without alias in the same patents. The trial was not merged into r6, because the gain is below 0.05% of the data while it would change the test sets again and invalidate the baselines.
   - The repair covers only MOL files with alias lines and reversed nitriles. A scan with the public databases found no further widespread structure problems among molecules without alias lines; this is not an exhaustive proof. About 400 records whose `(S)` stereo text was exported as a free S atom were not processed (the parent molecule is not affected).
   - Outside the patents covered by BindingDB and ChEMBL (about 60% of the patents, half of the records) the repaired structures are supported only by the rules and by the agreement of two implementations. `F2^C` read as CHF2 is the least certain rule (see [VALIDATION](VALIDATION.md)).
   - The nitrile rule of the conversion script is global. For records outside the 7,773 reviewed files, the evidence gate `verified_nitrile_guard.py` should be used instead of the global rule.
7. **The target mapping covers only part of the groups.** It covers 86.8% of the (patent, target) pairs (11,761 of 13,557). `target_mapped` is true on 350,664 rows (58.1%) and requires a single wild-type protein at high or medium confidence. The task sets use only `target_mapped` groups (`rank_complex_mutant` excepted).
   - For the 915 ranking-task groups (325 UniProt accessions), the sequence is the full-length sequence in 835 groups, the first subunit of a complex in 47 (`complex_primary_subunit`), a named mutant with the substitution applied in 25 and a mature chain in 8. The public-database cross-reference agrees for 490 groups, disagrees for 19 and is absent for 406.
   - A manual spot check of 165 entries (r4) is too small for an accuracy estimate with a confidence interval.
   - Free-text targets ("species|name") were assigned by UniProt search. Three such accessions were corrected in r5. `eval_support/suspect_free_text_mappings_outside_tasks.tsv` lists 23 further groups (7 accessions) outside the tasks whose accession does not fit the name; they are reported and unchanged, and the list is not an exhaustive scan.
8. **Patent families are a heuristic**, not an INPADOC lookup: same endpoint and molecule overlap of at least 50%. Tautomers are not unified, and parent standardisation only removes salts and neutralises charges.
9. **The unit check depends on the header text.** A table without a unit in its header and without public values to compare against cannot be checked for unit errors. Censored values (>, <) and graded values stay in `sar_all.parquet` but are not in the task sets.
10. **At the level of `sar_all.parquet`, a few (molecule, target) pairs occur in both train and test:** 152 of 58,607 among `target_mapped` rows (0.26%), coming from different patents and families. In the task files the number is 0 (verified on r4 and r5, whose test sets are identical; not repeated on r6).
11. **One patent in the old SGM format (US6211376B1) is not parsed** by the XML parser; it was not covered by the back-check, which is not a failed check.
12. **A comparison with the public databases cannot reveal an error that BindingDB shares with the pipeline.** BindingDB curates US patents too; if both read the same place wrongly (US10966980), the comparison shows no difference. Agreement on the overlapping part shows that the extraction is accurate; it is not evidence that the experiments are reproducible. The validation of the structure repair uses the same databases and has the same blind spot (168 reversed nitriles matched before the repair because the databases carry the same drawing).
13. **Assay noise limits every within-group metric.** The median max-min spread of repeated exact measurements of one molecule in one group is 0.504 log in the r6 manifest (0.498 in an earlier build). This statistic includes spreads above 1 log and therefore overstates the noise of the retained task records. For the 1,949 `rank_ic50` molecules measured twice, the median difference is 0.27 log (about 0.29 log per measurement). With 0.3 log per measurement, the median ceiling of the within-group Spearman correlation of a perfect model is about 0.88, and 11.6% of the `rank_ic50` groups have a ceiling below 0.6 (from the r6 freeze note).

## Protein structures

14. **The 3D structures are incomplete.** For the 915 ranking-task groups, the recommended structure is experimental for 867 groups, an AlphaFold model for 43 and missing for 5 (cytomegalovirus DNA polymerase UL54: 4 groups; *Ustilago maydis* acetyl-CoA carboxylase: 1 group). Among the 910 groups with a structure, the recommended structure covers less than half of the group sequence in 379 groups. The pocket is defined by one co-crystallised ligand and can differ from the binding mode of the patent series. AlphaFold models contain no ligand and no cofactor. Two groups use a homologue stand-in (O91080, sequence identity 86.6%, usable as a shape reference only); the structures of EZH2 Y641F and Y641N are wild-type; the R71H substitution of Q86WV6 is not resolved in its structure. For complexes only the first subunit is used (47 groups).
15. **Ligand leakage.** The co-crystallised ligand of a structure can be a PatBench compound. Of 463 holo structures, 59 have a ligand that occurs in PatBench (r5: 51). Among the 205 `rank_ic50` and `rank_ki_kd` test groups, the ligand of the recommended structure is also a test molecule of the same target in 10 groups, counting the test molecules of any task; counting only the test molecules of these two tasks, it is 5 groups (r5: 7 and 2; the counts grew because only repaired structures allow the crystal ligand to be matched with PatBench molecules). Models that use structures should exclude these groups or report them separately (`docking_eligible` and `structure_ligand_leak` in `eval_support/test_group_profile`; `recommended_ligand_in_group` in `structures/group_structure_map`). `docking_eligible` is true for 167 of the 205 groups; the other 38 have no structure (2), an AlphaFold model (14), a PDB structure without ligand (11), a sequence mismatch (1) or ligand leakage (10). Under the structure policy of the frozen evaluation run, structure-based models can be run on 131 of 172 `rank_ic50` and 23 of 33 `rank_ki_kd` test groups, and models of different types should be compared only on the common groups.
16. **A short section of `fetch_structures.py` has no established author.** The lines that assign `segment_map` and `META` (around lines 657-660) could not be attributed (see [CODE_PROVENANCE](CODE_PROVENANCE.md)). The output was checked independently on r4. The structure rows added in r5 and r6 did not receive that independent check; they passed `check_structures.py` only.

## Evaluation

17. **There is no evaluation script for the regression task.** `evaluate.py` handles only ranking; `regression_affinity` has to be scored by the user. The baselines of r6 cover the four ranking tasks `rank_ic50`, `rank_ki_kd`, `rank_highcontrast` and `rank_complex_mutant`; `rank_ec50` and `regression_affinity` have none.
18. **The build-time `evaluate.py` has limited statistics.** It resamples groups, not patents (see item 5), and it has no paired comparison between models on the same groups and no stratified report. The `target_seen_in_train_same_task` flag of `eval_support/test_group_profile` marks test groups whose target occurs in training groups of the same task (r6: 78 of 172 `rank_ic50` test groups and 15 of 33 `rank_ki_kd` test groups); the script does not use it. The freeze note of r6 names a separate evaluator (`recommended_evaluator` in `FROZEN.json`, sha256 `35b70dd019dc8da9...`) that resamples patents, compares models on the same groups, reports strata and does not impute failed predictions. That file is part of this repository, byte for byte, as `pipeline/10_evaluation/evaluate_patent_bootstrap.py` (`patbench run evaluate`); a test checks it against the recorded hash. The build-time `evaluate.py` remains because the leaderboard script imports it.
19. **Reference baselines (r6).** Zero-shot, time-split test, all test groups (not stratified by `test_tier`); median within-group Spearman correlation, with the 95% interval from group resampling. ECFP4 + random forest is trained on the train split of the task as a global regression on group-centred pAct, without tuning.

    | Task (test groups) | Heavy atoms | logP | ECFP4 + random forest |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | `rank_ic50` (172) | 0.090 [0.030, 0.180] | 0.083 [0.053, 0.137] | 0.072 [0.040, 0.095] |
    | `rank_ki_kd` (33) | 0.253 [0.144, 0.346] | 0.260 [0.118, 0.318] | 0.092 [0.061, 0.165] |
    | `rank_highcontrast` (182) | 0.092 [0.041, 0.189] | 0.085 [0.053, 0.133] | 0.045 [0.015, 0.074] |
    | `rank_complex_mutant` (29) | 0.164 [0.019, 0.252] | 0.171 [-0.004, 0.235] | -0.024 [-0.166, 0.044] |

    Heavy atoms and logP are not below ECFP4 + random forest in any of the four tasks; on `rank_ic50` the intervals of ECFP4 + random forest and of heavy atoms overlap. A model should therefore be judged by whether it **beats heavy-atom count and logP outside the interval**, not only by being above 0. The baseline table of r4 is not comparable with this one: the test groups and the training data differ. The effect of the structure repair on ECFP4 + random forest was not isolated, because no control with repaired structures and unchanged test groups was run.
20. **The few-shot ECFP4 + random forest predictions of r6 are invalid.** `baselines.py` overwrote the predictions for k = 5 and k = 10 with the zero-shot predictions. The eight files `baselines/<task>_ecfp_rf_k5.parquet` and `baselines/<task>_ecfp_rf_k10.parquet` and the corresponding rows of `baselines/leaderboard.csv` are invalid (`baselines/FEWSHOT_RF_INVALID_WARNING.txt`). The zero-shot columns and the k = 5 and k = 10 predictions of the random, heavy-atom and logP baselines are not affected. `baselines.py` and `leaderboard.py` in the r6 release are byte-identical to the r4 versions. `pipeline/10_evaluation/baselines.py` is that script as it ran and keeps the defect; `pipeline/10_evaluation/baselines_fixed.py` is the corrected copy (the zero-shot forest on the few-shot queries is written as `ecfp_rf_zero_k<K>`) and is what `patbench run baselines` and `patbench release` use. The corrected predictions for r6 were produced outside the frozen directory, in `patbench-r6-fewshot-baselines-20261101` (few-shot ECFP4 + random forest, median within-group Spearman on `rank_ic50`: 0.196 for k = 5 and 0.290 for k = 10, against 0.072 zero-shot; patent-resampled 95% intervals in that directory's README).

## Repository

21. **The upstream stages were not re-run in the repository.** Extraction, recovery and the structure catalogue (345,940 scanned patent archives) were not re-run. The code hashes recorded at run time document which code produced the tables (see [CODE_PROVENANCE](CODE_PROVENANCE.md)); that a re-run gives the same tables has not been verified.
22. **Curated data and stage outputs are not in the repository:** the curated results of the target mapping, the annotation files of the validation sample, and the outputs of the stages (see [CODE_PROVENANCE](CODE_PROVENANCE.md)). The curation steps of the target mapping and of the validation can therefore not be reproduced from the repository alone.
23. **r6 was not rebuilt from this repository.** r6 was built on the original server with the scripts stored in the r6 release (`scripts/`). A rebuild of r6 with this repository is not recorded in these documents. The rebuild comparison in [VALIDATION](VALIDATION.md) concerns r4 and an earlier snapshot of the repository.
24. **Licence and deposit.** The code is under the MIT License (`LICENSE`). The r6 documentation does not state a data licence (the r4 documentation planned CC BY 4.0 for the release directory). No deposit with a DOI (figshare or Zenodo) has been made.
